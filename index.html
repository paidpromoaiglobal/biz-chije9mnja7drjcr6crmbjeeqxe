Our Story

Made with Passion

Das Puja Bhandar is Jorhat's beloved one-stop shop for all your religious and festive needs, conveniently located in City Plaza Market, opposite Eleye Multiplex at Gar-Ali. Whether you are preparing for a grand puja ceremony, a seasonal festival, or everyday worship at home, we bring you a carefully curated collection of puja samagri, incense, idols, diyas, flowers, and more — all under one roof. Our commitment to quality and devotion has made us a trusted name among the families of Jorhat and beyond.

At Das Puja Bhandar, we understand that every ritual holds deep meaning and deserves the finest ingredients. That is why we source authentic products that honour tradition while catering to the modern devotee. Open six days a week from 10:30 AM to 10:00 PM and on Sundays from 5:00 PM to 9:00 PM, we are always here when you need us most — especially during those important auspicious occasions. Step into our store and experience the warmth, guidance, and spiritual atmosphere that our valued customers have come to love.
